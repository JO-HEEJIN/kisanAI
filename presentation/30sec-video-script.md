---

**VISUAL:** Smartphone screen showing AR mode / NASA satellite animation

## Filming Tips

1. **Use Real Footage**: Actual app demo on phone (not mockups)
2. **Show Before/After**: Dry field → AR scan → thriving crops
3. **Visual Data**: Animate NASA satellite data overlays
4. **Keep Text Minimal**: Max 3-5 words on screen at once
5. **Music**: Uplifting, tech-forward background track
6. **Captions**: Add for accessibility (many judges watch muted)
