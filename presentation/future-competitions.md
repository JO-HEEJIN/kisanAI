## Technical Modifications Needed

### High Priority (Apply to Most Competitions):
1. **Multi-satellite support**: Add Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, ISRO satellites
2. **Multilingual UI**: Hindi, Spanish, French, Amharic
3. **Offline-first optimization**: Reduce bandwidth, extend cache to 7 days
4. **Carbon tracking module**: Soil organic carbon, sequestration estimation
5. **Regional crop database**: Expand beyond current 20 crops to 100+

### Medium Priority (Competition-specific):
6. **EU CAP indicators**: For European Big Data Hackathon
7. **Azure deployment**: For Microsoft Imagine Cup
8. **Disease detection**: For CIP AI-griculture Hackathon
9. **Financial modules**: Carbon credits, micro-insurance for NABARD
10. **Policy dashboards**: For World Bank/UNDP competitions

### Low Priority (Nice-to-have):
11. **IoT sensor integration**: For Cornell/academic competitions
